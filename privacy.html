Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Introduction

Climberforsuccess LLC ("we", "us", "our") operates FinanceAI Pro. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Financial data: transactions, card details (last 4 digits only), balances, debts
  • Payment data: processed securely by Stripe — we never store full card numbers
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, device type, browser
  • Uploaded files: receipt images uploaded for AI scanning

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Service
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To generate AI-powered financial insights
  • To send important account notifications
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Supabase — database and authentication provider
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • OpenAI — AI-powered receipt scanning and financial insights
  • Vercel — hosting and deployment

All third-party providers are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection laws.

5. Data Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/SSL encryption
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Financial card data stores only the last 4 digits
  • We follow industry best practices to protect your data

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

FinanceAI Pro is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via email or in-app notification.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests:

Política de Privacidad

Última actualización: Junio 2025

1. Introducción

Climberforsuccess LLC ("nosotros") opera FinanceAI Pro. Esta Política de Privacidad explica cómo recopilamos, usamos y protegemos tu información personal.

2. Información que Recopilamos
